Privacy Policy — Hearing Aid

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Hearing Aid is a hearing-assistance application for iPhone, iPad and Android developed by ShinetechData ("we"). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the application.

Summary

  • Microphone audio is processed inside your device. It is not recorded, stored or sent to any server.
  • We do not ask for an account, email or personal data to use the app.
  • We do not show advertising and do not use third-party trackers.
  • Only microphone permission is requested (and, on Android, notifications for the background-service notice).

1. What data we process

1.1 Microphone audio

The app captures the ambient sound to amplify and equalize it in real time. All processing happens entirely on your device. Audio is not stored and is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

1.2 Local preferences

We store minimal operating data on your own device, such as whether you already saw the welcome screen and your light/dark theme preference. This information does not leave the device.

1.3 In-app purchases (only if the subscription is active)

If you subscribe, the payment is processed by Apple (App Store) on iOS or Google Play on Android. To validate access we use RevenueCat, which may process a purchase or device identifier to confirm whether your subscription is active. We never receive your card details.

1.4 Diagnostics and advertising

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

3. Security

The app does not transmit your audio or personal data. Preference data stays in the app's private storage on your device.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Your rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no information to delete on our servers. You can revoke permissions at any time in the system settings and uninstall the app to erase the preferences stored on the device.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or the law. We will publish the current version at this same address and state the update date.
