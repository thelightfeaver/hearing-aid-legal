Frequently asked questions

Why do I hear an echo or a whistle?

Use headphones. With the speaker, the microphone picks up the amplified sound again and feedback (echo) occurs.

Does Hearing Aid replace a hearing aid?

No. It is a help to hear ambient sound better and does not replace a hearing aid, an audiological assessment or a medical diagnosis. If you have doubts about your hearing, consult a health professional.

How do I stop the amplification?

With the power button on the main screen. On Android, also with the Stop button in the notification.

Is my audio recorded or sent anywhere?

No. Audio is processed inside your phone and is never recorded or transmitted. See the privacy policy.

Does it work in the background?

Yes, with the screen off or while using other apps, as long as the system does not close the app for lack of resources.
